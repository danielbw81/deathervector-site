      The engines

      Three styles, three separate accounts.

      Each engine trades its own paper account with its own daily loss limit. No human picks the trades.

      Scalper

      Minutes to hours

      Intraday trades in the day's strongest-moving stocks, with automated exits. Flat by the end of every session.

      Quant

      Monthly

      A momentum portfolio of US stocks, rebalanced at the start of each month at the opening auction.

      Longterm

      Quarterly

      Half an S&P 500 index fund, half 25 large companies ranked on quality and value. Rebalanced each quarter. First buys December 1, 2026.

      How we report

      The rules for this page.

      1. Everything is shown. Every trade from the first day, winners and losers. Nothing is removed or re-run. Test trades made before the record started are listed separately under the ledger.
      2. Straight from the system. Numbers come from the trading system's own fill records and the broker's reported account values, once a day after the close.
      3. Dated and public. Each daily update is a dated commit in a public repository, so earlier days can't be quietly edited.
      4. Paper, said plainly. These are paper accounts: simulated money at real market prices. Real-money results can differ, for example in fills on fast-moving stocks.
      5. Events explained. When something goes wrong or a rule changes, a dated entry under Results says what happened and what was done.
      6. Compared with the market. Results sit next to the S&P 500 (SPY) over the same period.
